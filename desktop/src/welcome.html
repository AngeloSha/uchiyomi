How do you want to use Uchiyomi?

You can switch later from the Uchiyomi icon (in the system tray on Windows, the menu bar on a Mac).

Connect to your Uchiyomi server

Type the address you open Uchiyomi at in your browser.

For example https://manga.example.com or http://192.168.1.10:8080
Over plain http, chapters cannot be saved for offline reading. An https address is recommended.
